FAQ

What is managed GEO?
Managed GEO is a service where Anscite's team runs generative engine optimization for you, so your brand appears in AI answers. We handle content creation, detection questions, and reporting on a fixed schedule.
How does the free probe work?
We pick 5–8 questions, ask two or more AI models, and give you a baseline report. There is no charge and no contract required.
What are the plans and prices?
The Basic plan is ¥2,980 per month with 4 new content pieces, 20 detection questions, and one report per month. The Standard plan is ¥5,980 per month with 12 new content pieces, 50 detection questions, and a report every two weeks. Both are billed monthly with a 3-month minimum.
