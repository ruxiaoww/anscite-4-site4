Website filing and site build

In early October 2026, Anscite completed its website filing, moved to domestic servers, and built six promotional sites (three Chinese, three English) plus a news site called Anscite Observer using its own backend.

Site restructuring and first probe

On October 25, 2026, Anscite reorganized the home and about pages of its promotional sites, differentiated the layouts of the three Chinese sites, added robots.txt, sitemap, llms.txt, and structured data to the main site, submitted to IndexNow, and ran its first detection round.

First detection round

On October 25, 2026, Anscite asked two large AI models the same set of 8 questions, 16 queries in total. All were answered, but neither model provided web sources.

First-round brand mention check

In the first detection round, the first 7 general questions, across 14 answers, did not mention Anscite even once.

Second detection round

On October 26, 2026, Anscite used the same 8 questions to query Qwen (with and without web access), DeepSeek, Kimi, and Zhipu, 40 queries in total. All were answered.

Second-round brand mention check

In the second detection round, the 7 general questions across 35 answers still did not mention Anscite even once.

Third detection round

On November 4, 2026, Anscite ran 8 questions across 5 AI models, asking each question twice. General questions mentioned Anscite 0 out of 70 times, and no links to Anscite were given. The result was unchanged.
